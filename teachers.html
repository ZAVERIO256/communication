Suggested professional practices

  • Begin with the learning outcome before choosing a digital tool.
  • Test equipment and open files before the lesson begins.
  • Prepare offline alternatives where internet access is unreliable.
  • Protect learner information and follow school ICT policies.
  • Evaluate whether technology improved learner participation or understanding.
