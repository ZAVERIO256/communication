About the Hub

The Digital Learning & Teacher Resource Hub is a proposed district-level educational portal designed to make selected digital learning and teaching resources easier to find and use. It is intended for secondary school students, subject teachers, parents and education administrators in Masaka District.

Core objectives

  • Virtual Library & Open Educational Resources: organise digital textbooks, notes, syllabuses and revision materials.
  • Teachers' Professional Development: share pedagogical guidance, teaching aids and digital lesson-planning resources.
  • Interactive Student Corner: provide subject learning support, educational videos, revision activities and career guidance.
  • Administrative & Community Noticeboard: provide a central place for approved announcements, examination information and useful contact details.

Who can use the portal?

Students

Access study materials, watch lessons and practise key concepts.

Teachers

Use teaching guides, lesson-planning ideas and professional resources.

Parents & Administrators

Find learning information, view approved notices and contact the hub.
